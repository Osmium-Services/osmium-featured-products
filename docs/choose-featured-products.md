**Featured Products** is a hand-picked list of up to five products, which your site's design shows where it chooses, such as the homepage.

## Pick the products

1. Open **Featured Products**.
2. Click a slot to choose a product for it. Click an empty slot to add one.
3. Drag the slots to change the order.
4. **Clear Slot** empties a slot.

## If a product will not appear in the list

A product can only be featured when all of these are true:

- Its status is **Available**.
- It has a price greater than zero.
- It has not been deleted.
- It has a product page that exists and is not deleted.

## Warnings

A message appears if a featured product has a problem and will not display properly, or if fewer than five slots are filled. Replace any product with issues.

## Two people editing

Only one person can edit at a time. If someone else is editing, you are shown who and when they started.
